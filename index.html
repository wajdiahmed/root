01 / WHOAMI

Both sides of the firewall

I'm an offensive security professional who turns adversary thinking into measurable risk reduction. Companies bring me in to simulate real attacks, expose what's exploitable, and close it — before it becomes an incident, a breach, or a headline.

Over 4+ years I've worked both sides of the firewall: leading red team engagements and penetration tests, while also running the SOC operations, incident response, and threat hunting that defend live environments. That dual perspective means I don't just report findings — I understand how they get detected, exploited, and fixed in practice.

I've also helped organizations achieve ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certification by implementing the technical controls auditors actually look for, and I build the automation and tooling that lets small security teams operate at scale. The outcome for the business: fewer blind spots, faster response, and security that holds up under real scrutiny.
